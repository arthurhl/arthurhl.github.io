04Proof, not buzzwords

What I like to
untangle.

A few real constraints I’ve had to respect — and the engineering choices that made them manageable.

01 / performance
O(n²)
→ O(n)

Remove the bottleneck before it becomes an incident.

A document-related path had nested processing that would grow badly with each related record. I reduced its algorithmic complexity proactively, before volume turned it into a production problem.

02 / document systems
100%
no longer the norm

Rebuild the path, not just the symptom.

A company-wide monthly invoice used to push database utilization to 100%. Reworking the data, query, event, and caching path stopped the recurring saturation without changing the billing outcome.

03 / field reality
Offline
by design

Connectivity is a constraint, not a footnote.

For public-sector teams working in the field, the mobile flow kept local SQLite data useful offline and synchronized through REST when a connection returned.
